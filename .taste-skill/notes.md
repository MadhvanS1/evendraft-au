# Evendraft AU redesign: notes

## Reading of the brief
Owner (15 Nov 2026): the first version is "too simple"; he wants it as rich as Evendraft UK and JaagteRaho. Same brand as
Evendraft UK, so its palette (stone, paper, ink, deep green, plus its amber/blue/green tints) and faces (Newsreader +
Source Sans 3, self-hosted) are kept. Premium = few elements, each beautifully made; product fragments; strong colour; motion.

## Inventory (only this client)
1. AusTender closes every Commonwealth tender in "ACT Local Time" (Canberra), printed under each closing time.
2. Australia's split clocks: in summer Canberra/Sydney/Melbourne/Hobart are UTC+11, Adelaide +10:30, Brisbane +10,
   Darwin +9:30, Perth +8. QLD, WA, NT never move their clocks.
3. The desk runs from Bengaluru (UTC+5:30): 5h30 behind Canberra in summer, 4h30 in winter.
4. AusTender's own artefacts: ATM ID, "Close Date & Time", "ATM Type: Request for Tender", "Conditions for Participation",
   addenda, the lodgement receipt ("save and print this receipt as proof of lodgement"), late tenders not accepted.
5. Australian must-haves: ABN, $20m public liability, WHS management system, Working with Children checks, DISP for defence.
6. Eight state/territory portals plus AusTender (names: buy.nsw, Buying for Victoria, QTenders, Tenders WA, ...).
7. Canberra itself: Walter Burley Griffin's plan, Marion Mahony Griffin's renderings (1911-12), Old Parliament House (1927).
8. The second product's world: working bees, sausage sizzles ("democracy sausage" at polling places), P&C trivia nights,
   street Christmas parties, council road-closure permits, SPF 50+.
9. The Evendraft promise: "Every line has a source"; AI-assisted drafting, the client approves every claim; free memo.
10. The person: Madhvan Sharma, run from India (as disclosed on the UK site).

## Idea
On this page, AusTender's rule that every Commonwealth tender closes on Canberra time becomes the headline you can type:
write a closing time the way AusTender prints it and watch it land on every Australian clock (and Bengaluru's), with
the back-plan to lodgement.
Competitor test: a UK bid writer cannot say this; a generic AU bid writer could show a timeline, but not the live
Canberra-clock converter built on AusTender's own convention plus the desk's Bengaluru clock.
Live thing: that converter (showcase section). Plain fallback without script: the static example answer stays visible.

## Argument (home)
- Hero: Evendraft AU writes tender answers for Australian businesses. Proof: the example memo mosaic + live Canberra clock.
- Showcase: closing times are Canberra's, not yours. Proof: type a closing time, see it everywhere + the back-plan.
- How it works: six stages from link to lodgement, each with the document we hand over. Proof: the fragments.
- Every line has a source: the example memo, each line traced to the clause it came from. Proof: the memo itself.
- About: who runs it, from where, how AI is used, what you approve. Proof: plain facts, highlighted.
- Price: the memo is free; everything else quoted in AUD first. Proof: the ticket.
- Events: the community side. Proof: a real photograph + link to the drafting tool.
- FAQ: real questions (login, lodging, state tenders, AI, cost).
- Contact: the memo form (works, posts to the Evendraft CRM).
- Footer: the name at full width (brand signature), credits for found pictures.

## Redraws and deviations (for the report)
- Hero: kept the brand's own mosaic hero instead of the dealt type-only colour field, because the owner explicitly asked
  for a hero like Evendraft UK / JaagteRaho (product fragments).
- Contact: dealt "address in display rows" needs an address, hours and phone that don't exist; built the memo form as the
  contact chapter instead.
- Footer: name at full width (brand signature) instead of an email line; no public email on this site by choice.
- Features ("sentences with switches") dropped: the argument has no claim for it; the events chapter takes its slot.
