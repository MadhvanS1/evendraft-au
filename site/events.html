      Small things it gets right

      • Queensland, Western Australia and the Northern Territory keep standard time all summer, so each draft follows its own state's clock.
      • A working bee opens with a welcome, an Acknowledgement of Country and a safety briefing before anyone picks up a rake.
      • A street party needs a council road-closure permit, and the shopping list says to apply at least six weeks ahead.
      • Sausage sizzle quantities allow for the customers who come back for a second snag.
      • The RSVP date lands five days before the event, so there is time to buy the right amount.
