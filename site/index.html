Bid, with two conditions

For Ridgeline Grounds: 22 staff, three crews, based in the region.

Why bid

  • Mowing, slashing and fire-break work at four sites is your core trade, at a size a small firm can serve. SoR 3
  • Local benefit is a tenth of the score, and all three crews live in the region. RS D

Conditions, before questions close

  • Raise public liability to $20 million, or get a broker's letter that it will be in place before award. DC 14.2
  • Name two similar contracts from the last five years, each with a referee. RS C

Why be careful

  • Every depot is priced separately, so one wrong site estimate shows up in the score. PS 1
  • The site visit on 19 Nov is optional, but it is the only look at the depots before you price. Add 1

Who you'd work with

Evendraft AU is the Australian desk of Evendraft, which writes tender answers for UK companies. Questions

Do you need my AusTender login?

No. Send the link. If the documents sit behind the portal login, download them and forward them by email. We never ask for passwords.

Do you lodge the bid for us?

No. You lodge on the portal from your own account and keep the lodgement receipt. The declarations are yours to sign.

Is the memo really free?

Yes, whether or not you bid, and whether or not you work with us afterwards.

Which tenders do you cover?

Commonwealth tenders on AusTender, and state and territory tenders on their own portals: buy.nsw, Buying for Victoria, QTenders, Tenders WA, SA Tenders and Contracts, and the Tasmanian, ACT and Northern Territory portals.

How do you use AI?

AI tools read the documents and draft against the criteria. A person checks every line against its source, and you approve every claim. Nothing goes into a bid that you haven't seen.

What time zone do you work in?

The desk is in Bengaluru, five and a half hours behind Canberra in summer and four and a half in winter. Closing times are always handled in ACT local time, the way AusTender prints them.

What does a full bid cost?

It depends on the size of the tender and the number of criteria. We quote in Australian dollars after the memo, before any work starts.

Can you help with a panel or standing offer?

Yes. Panels are worth the effort because agencies buy from them for years. The memo says whether this panel suits your work.
