What's in a memo

One page you can read in five minutes, built from the tender's own documents.

  • The conditions for participation, checked against your business. Miss one and the bid is out before it's scored.
  • How the bid will be scored, with the weightings the documents give.
  • The closing time in your own time zone. AusTender closes in ACT local time, so in summer a 2:00 pm close is 1:00 pm in Brisbane and 11:00 am in Perth.
  • The paperwork to gather now: insurance certificates, policies, referees, the pricing schedule.
  • Our call, bid or don't, and why. A no costs you nothing either.
